Gerry's Tray, on his phone

Nine jobs sit in Gerry's pricing tray. The oldest has waited 35 days. The money is earned; it just can't be invoiced until Gerry prices it.

This alpha keeps pricing with Gerry. It drafts the number the way he prices, from the WhatsApp, the lad's sheet and a 20-second voice note. Gerry nudges it, approves it, sends it.

  1. Tap a job in the tray.
  2. Hold the amber button and "talk" (the note is scripted).
  3. Check the draft, change any line, approve.

Prototype for teaching. Case C is fictional, every price is invented, and no AI runs here: the "brain" is a script that pretends it has learned Gerry's patterns.
